# Wave 2: integration of wave 1

All eight wave 1 branches are merged into `master` with their history (real merges, `--no-ff`): net, dancers, worlds,
field and steam, fire and plasma, water and ice, audio, then portals (which arrived during the work and went in after
the seven). The seams between them are joined: one power form per dancer, presence from the show, fire lighting the
worlds, matter voices, the dancers' physics on the worlds' real stages, the played dancer online, steam after the depth
of field, loadout presets with one `?dress`, and every kind honouring the portals' per-view `seen` and `carry`.

Measured: after every merge `npm run typecheck`, `npm run build`, the tests and a headless still on the RTX 5090; the
whole `npm test` (limits, src tests, audio tests, the gates' algebra, the net tests) at the end; every still below
captured and looked at; GPU timings per pass for the fire and water pair in the geode and the redwoods; probes of the
field, the matter voices' event counts, the physics' positions on ledges and through a gate read back from the page;
the played dancer online in two headless browsers. Not measured: anything by ear, a phone, a real gamepad or webcam,
a real network, a long soak.

Captures ran against a production build served by `vite preview` on port 5392 (built into the scratchpad), so editing
`src/` during a capture never reloaded the page; the dev server was used for the first merges on 5391. The room server
for the net check ran on 5393. All three are stopped. Every capture took the shared render lock (it was often held by
other agents: waits of minutes).

## Open items, most important first

1. **The uniform budget is full.** The world light group is six uniforms in every lit shader; the gems' draw now
   declares 12 of the baseline's 12 per stage (water drops and ice shards 11). It ran on the 5090; a new world, or any
   new uniform in a lit shader, breaks the baseline. Pack the five worlds' uniforms and the fire light into one buffer
   (a world indexes its own part) before wave 2 adds anything to `light.wgsl`.
2. **Water through a gate.** A gate's view has no water surface (its passes run after the gate is shown). Either run
   the surface's passes per gate view inside the `gates` stage (the frame's ordering of a kind's items per view), or
   accept drops and ice only.
3. **The field is one world's.** With dancers in two worlds only the main view's have a field; steam, mist and arcs are
   main-view-only. A field per world (or per live view) is a real design decision.
4. **Fire's smoke over water** reads as dark round sprites on the water body; the field's wet was zero until now, so
   nobody had seen it. The fire's owner should look (its `smoke` parameter, the sprite's shape).
5. **Steam** saturates where fire or plasma meets ice (a white cloud fills a close frame) even at density 0.12, and is
   crisper than the blurred frame round it (it is drawn after the lens). A softer edge or a little blur of its own.
6. **Not seen yet**: flame bending behind a kick, water thinning in heat, water freezing beside ice, ice melting beside
   fire, set ice melting from the field's heat (`setIce.warm` is called only for a networked melt; the field's heat
   still doesn't melt set ice by itself), the geode heart and the other presets, falling off the forge in free play.
   A short film of each, looked at.
7. **Network**: the set pieces → set ice path and the `travel` message are written but nothing sends `build`, and the
   two-browser check did not cross a gate. The receiving side of `travel` relies on the remote's own poses (no
   `gates.travel` call); the brief moment between a remote crossing and its first carried pose was not measured. The
   music clock follows the room only through `FrameCtx.beat` (the audio engine's own `syncTo` is not wired).
8. **The follow camera after a crossing** turns by the player's yaw in the far world's frame (it swings toward the
   wrong side once the player runs); the rig has no notion of "the player is in another world".
9. **A dark smear on the geode's mirror floor** under a fire dancer (two stills), not traced.
10. **Dark needle streaks** (probably the gems' spare stones) streak through stills where nobody wears gems (dark needles), and the sparkles show in every
   world's view (the portals' known gap).
11. **Firelight per world**: the fire light uniform is the main view's; a gate's view of another world gets no
    firelight from the dancers there.
12. `npm test`'s net tests start their own server on port 5418 (outside this session's 5391–5399; a test fixture, left
    as it is).
13. The brightness step at a gate's face (7 to 25 %, the portals' report): unchanged.
