# The music, from Suno

The procedural score (src/audio) is being replaced by tracks made on Suno (v6, Advanced, lyrics left empty so each is
an instrumental). One track per world, each in the key and tempo of the mood that world already plays (src/audio/sound.ts,
harmony.ts MOODS), so the dancers' own pitched sounds (stones, matter voices) stay in key and the jogo's beat can follow
the track. The temple's track is film 2's score (90 bpm) and the redwoods' is film 1's (112 bpm).

Exclude styles (More Options): `vocals, lyrics, rap, EDM drop, distorted guitar`

## Moon Over the Tide Temple (temple; film 2), 90 bpm, G major

Cinematic capoeira instrumental, 90 BPM, G major. Berimbau gunga sets a hypnotic pulse, pandeiro and atabaque in a soft
angola rhythm, agogo accents, palmas on the backbeat. Over it: warm solo cello, soft string pads, glassy celesta, a
distant wordless choir like moonlight on water, slow tidal swells, gentle handpan. Starts sparse and meditative, builds
to a luminous full roda with soaring strings, a hushed breakdown, then a radiant final swell and a soft ending. Mystical,
elemental, fire meeting water, beautiful and hopeful. Spacious stone-hall reverb, high-fidelity film score mix.

## Embers in the Redwoods (redwoods; film 1), 112 bpm, D minor

Mystical forest-at-dusk dance, instrumental, 112 BPM, D minor, gently swung. Berimbau and atabaque groove with shakers
and frame drum, plucked kalimba and nylon-string guitar ostinato, warm crackling ember textures, deep felt bass, lush
string pads and a solo cello melody, breathy shakuhachi flute, glockenspiel like fireflies. Opens alone in a clearing,
grows into a dancing groove, then a magical swell where two bodies fuse into an aurora (shimmering choir pad, rising
strings), then settles back to the forest. Warm, golden, enchanted, organic, cinematic, wide and intimate.

## Inside the Geode (the geode and the hub: the first thing heard), 112 bpm, A minor

Crystal cave ambient groove, instrumental, 112 BPM, A minor. Glass marimba and crystal kalimba arpeggios, singing bowls,
deep sub-bass pulses, soft berimbau and hand percussion with long cave echoes, shimmering granular pads, bell tones like
gemstones catching light. Dreamy, wondrous, slowly building, a welcoming and mysterious opening theme. Long cave reverb,
glittering highs, warm lows, loop-friendly with no hard ending.

## The Still Pool (the pool), 120 bpm, C major

Moonlit still-water ambient dance, instrumental, 120 BPM in C major with a half-time feel. Water-drop percussion, soft
felt piano, harp arpeggios, airy synth pads, a slow rolling bass, light brushed hand drums, an ethereal wordless choir
swelling like rising steam. Serene, weightless, hopeful, glowing: a dance on black water under a full moon. Wide, clear,
lush reverb, loop-friendly with no hard ending.

## The Sky Forge (the forge), 124 bpm, F sharp minor

Epic tribal forge rhythm, instrumental, 124 BPM, F sharp minor. Anvil hits and metallic percussion, taiko and surdo
drums with atabaque, a driving berimbau ostinato, deep pulsing synth bass, molten brass swells, bright synth plucks like
sparks, low strings, rising tension and release. Powerful, fiery, heroic, dark and bright at once: a dance among floating
stone under a lava sky. Tight, punchy close room, cinematic, loop-friendly with no hard ending.
