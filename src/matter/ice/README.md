# matter/ice (wave 1: water + ice)

Bodies of ice, and ice that sets: shards on the capsules, and matter left behind on the beat that freezes into
bridges and ledges (building, see docs/PLAN.md). It implements `MatterKind` (src/matter/core/kind.ts) and registers
from an `index.ts` here; set ice becomes a world fixture (`World.fixtures`) and adds ledges to the stage planes.
