# matter/reactions (wave 1: field + reactions)

What happens where kinds meet, through the field (src/matter/field): steam (water in heat), smoke (flame in wet),
melting, freezing, quenching, and the fusion flows when two dancers fuse. A reaction is most likely its own
`MatterKind` (steam is matter too) that reads the field and the other kinds' particles; it registers from an
`index.ts` here like any kind.
